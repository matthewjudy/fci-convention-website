Convention and travel questions.

Some links are still coming soon and will be added as they are confirmed.

I own multiple territories/franchises. Will I be charged a convention fee for each location?

No. The convention fee will apply once in this circumstance. The registration fee includes 1 franchisee. Each additional attendee will have a separate fee.

What is included in the registration fee?

The registration fee includes what you will need while onsite during the convention dates, including food, drinks, attendance at convention-related events, the Welcome Party, vendor showcase, Closing Bash, swag gifts, and related convention programming.

What are the hotel costs?

These are the published convention planning rates. Confirm the final rate, occupancy terms, taxes, fees, and availability in the Hard Rock reservation flow.

  • Double occupancy$549
  • Single occupancy$479
  • Additional person in the room, maximum 4 per room$76
  • Children ages 13-17$76
  • Kids 12 and under sharing the room with their parents are $0 per child, per night (maximum 2 free kids per room)$0
How can I make a hotel reservation?

Hotel reservations are completed through the Hard Rock Riviera Maya group reservation link opens in a new tab.

How does the hotel charge work?

The hotel will charge three nights when the reservation is made. The remaining balance will be paid on arrival. Hotel reservations are completed through the Hard Rock Riviera Maya group reservation link opens in a new tab.

How can I change or update my existing hotel reservation?

Use My Bookings on the Hard Rock reservation site or call +52-984-875-1100. Changes depend on availability and are not permitted within 30 days of arrival. Transportation update instructions are coming soon.

What is the hotel cancellation policy?

Cancel at least 30 days before arrival to preserve the deposit. A no-show forfeits the full deposit.

What if the business cancels registration or does not attend?

If the business cancels and does not attend, the full convention fee will be charged.

How far is it from the airport to the hotel?

Airport transportation details and estimated travel time to Hard Rock Riviera Maya are coming soon.

What are the entry requirements?

The registration forms ask guests to confirm that their passport will remain valid for at least six months after the scheduled return date, or to indicate that a passport is in process. Guests are responsible for obtaining any required travel documents, visas, or passport renewals. Additional travel requirement details are coming soon.

What information will be listed on my badge?

Your badge will display your first and last name, branch name, and participant type. Badge information will reflect what you enter on your convention registration form, so please check spelling carefully.

What time zone is Hard Rock Riviera Maya in?

Hard Rock Riviera Maya is in the Cancun/Riviera Maya time zone: Eastern Standard Time, UTC-5, year-round. Cancun does not observe daylight saving time.

During convention dates, Cancun is the same time as Eastern Time, 1 hour ahead of Central Time, 2 hours ahead of Mountain Time, and 3 hours ahead of Pacific Time.
