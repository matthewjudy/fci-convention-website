## Overall Impression

The page now feels trustworthy, branded, and operationally honest. Its weakest moment is exactly where conversion should peak: the registration section asks visitors to absorb a caveat wall, choose among three attendee roles plus one non-attendee workflow, and wait for a large cross-origin form before seeing the escape route.

## What’s Working

- The real-world two-step truth—register each attendee, then reserve the hotel separately—is explicit and avoids false completion claims.
- Accessibility fundamentals are thoughtful: skip navigation, visible focus, semantic tabs, arrow-key behavior, reduced-motion handling, labeled dialogs, and direct-form recovery.
- Real imagery and verified event content create credibility without fabricated testimonials or outcomes.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Sponsorship Is Presented as a Registration Role

**Why it matters:** Diamond Tier appears beside Owner, Employee, and Vendor inside “Registration Forms.” A vendor can complete sponsorship intake and incorrectly believe an attendee badge was registered.

**Fix:** Keep three attendee tabs. Move Diamond Tier into a separate Vendor Sponsorship card with a persistent “Does Not Register Attendees” label and reciprocal Vendor Registration link.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden index.html`

### [P1] Register CTAs Bypass Essential Fee and Compliance Context

**Why it matters:** Hero and header Register links jump past the fee table and $1,999 nonattendance consequence. Discovering material obligations only after committing creates avoidable mistrust.

**Fix:** Repeat a compact, unchanged fee/compliance summary immediately before role selection, with a link back to full pricing details.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify index.html`

### [P1] Mobile Users Reach the Role Choice and Escape Route Too Late

**Why it matters:** At 390px, four guidance paragraphs precede the tabs, while the direct new-tab fallback follows a multi-thousand-pixel iframe. Slow-connection, keyboard, or assistive-technology users cannot choose the simpler route promptly.

**Fix:** Convert preparation guidance to three scannable bullets plus a disclosure; place each direct-form action before its iframe; retain loading and failure recovery.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt index.html`

### [P2] Multi-Attendee and Hotel Continuity Depends on Memory

**Why it matters:** The host cannot verify Jotform submission, but returning franchise owners still need a truthful way to track which attendee they handled and whether hotel booking remains.

**Fix:** Add a clearly manual, local-only completion checklist or printable handoff. State that checking an item records the visitor’s own progress and does not verify submission.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden index.html`

### [P2] Operational Focus Is Diluted by Page Volume and Wide Copy

**Why it matters:** Seven header links, repeated keynote proof, two galleries, thirteen FAQs, repeated CTAs, and 26 long-line detector hits make the registration-first experience longer and more interchangeable than necessary.

**Fix:** Narrow the primary navigation; constrain instructional measure; defer nonessential proof; rename “Location” to “Hotel”; keep critical operational content ahead of galleries.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable polish index.html`
